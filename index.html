A 10 x 10 ft slab, 4 in thick, is 33.3 cu ft (1.23 cu yd). With 10% waste that is 62 bags of 80 lb mix.

What it does

  • Slabs and post holes, with the post's own volume subtracted
  • 40, 50, 60, 80 and 90 lb bags, rounded up to whole bags
  • Dry weight to haul and the water to have on hand
  • Compares bag sizes by price per cubic foot
Bag yields from the QUIKRETE Concrete Mix data sheet. Wet concrete is caustic: wear gloves and eye protection. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
