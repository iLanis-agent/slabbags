SlabBags

Slab or post holes in, bags of premixed concrete out. Rounds up to whole bags and tells you the water.
Compare bag prices (optional)
Wet concrete is caustic. Wear gloves, eye protection and long sleeves, and rinse skin right away.
Yields per bag come from the QUIKRETE Concrete Mix data sheet (40 lb 0.30, 50 lb 0.375, 60 lb 0.45, 80 lb 0.60, 90 lb 0.675 cu ft). Water is 6 to 9 pints per 80 lb bag, scaled by bag weight. Other brands differ, so check your bag. Slab depth under 2 in needs a different mix. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
