# SlabBags

How many bags of premixed concrete for a slab or a set of post holes. Rounds up to whole bags, adds a waste allowance, shows dry weight and mixing water, and compares bag sizes by price per cubic foot.

- Live: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/slabbags/
- App: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/slabbags/app.html

Bag yields (40 lb 0.30, 50 lb 0.375, 60 lb 0.45, 80 lb 0.60, 90 lb 0.675 cu ft) and 6 to 9 pints of water per 80 lb bag are from the QUIKRETE Concrete Mix data sheet. Other brands differ. Wet concrete is caustic: wear gloves and eye protection.

Run tests: `node test-engine.js` (39 checks).
